# Riftbound Market Primer

*State of the market as of July 11, 2026. Prices are May–July 2026 snapshots from linked sources — treat as approximate and re-verify with `tracker/` data before trading.*

## Set calendar

Riot's cadence is **4 sets/year**. China launched first; the CN/EN gap shrank each set until full parity at Set 4.

## Rarity/chase architecture

Alt-arts → Epics (~1:4 packs) → **Overnumbered Legends** (numbered above set size, e.g. #299/298; ~1 per 3 displays) → **Signature Overnumbered** (foil artist signature; ~1:360 packs) → **Ultimate** (from UNL; <0.1% of packs). No serialized retail cards exist as of Set 3; the closest are CN tournament "Prestige" prize cards (a Prestige Battle Bunny Miss Fortune sold for ~$9,000 — the highest known Riftbound sale).

## Supply-side dynamics (the defining feature of this market)

- **Riot reprints aggressively** — stated policy: keep reprinting early sets "until the market reaches healthy quantities." Origins reprint waves landed Apr–May 2026 and cut box prices 45% in 9 weeks.
- **Reprints are indistinguishable from first prints** (different printing partner, but Riot blind-tested; no 1st-edition stamp). Some dealers (DA Card World) sell 1st print separately anyway — premium magnitude unquantified.
- **Spiritforged printed +50% over Origins and still sold out instantly**; bot-driven preorder chaos pushed Riot to a 48-hour lottery-drawing system on merch.riotgames.com with per-Riot-ID limits.
- **Riot kills speculative singles spikes**: after the March 2026 bans made Flash (Proving Grounds exclusive common) spike to $72, Riot shipped free Flash promos to every LGS. See `strategy-playbook.md`.
- Known quality event: Origins launch collation defect (some packs short on rares); Riot promised make-goods and fixes from Set 3.

## Demand-side evidence

- TCGplayer Q4 2025 bestsellers: **Riftbound debuted #5 among all TCGs**, beating Lorcana; held #5 in Q1 2026.
- ICv2 Spring 2026 hobby "Hot Properties": Riftbound #8.
- Every set launch so far has sold out despite growing print runs.
- Read: strongest new-TCG launch since Lorcana/One Piece; sealed speculation cooled hard post-reprints but player demand keeps growing.

## Grading

- PSA, BGS, CGC, SGC all grade Riftbound. Pop reports: PSA set pages, GemRate, PCG (pcgpopreport.com/RFTBD), and PriceCharting pop pages.
- Populations are tiny (single/double digits on most cards; Kai'Sa Signature: 378 graded total). First-mover advantage in grading chase signatures is still live.
- PSA cheapest tier $79.99/card (June 2026) — economics only work above ~$50 raw.

## Distribution

- Co-produced with **UVS Games** (organized play + retail network; store locator: locator.riftbound.uvsgames.com).
- Channels: LGS, Riot merch store direct (lottery drawings, ID limits), and full mass retail as of 2026 (Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Micro Center, Amazon; GameStop sells graded singles).
- **Arbitrage notes**: Chinese-language cards trade at a discount to English (magnitude unquantified); CN-exclusive items (Worlds venue bundles, New Year packs, Prestige cards) carry big Western premiums. EU/US MSRP roughly at parity.
