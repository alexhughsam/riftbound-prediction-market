## Account-by-account

### @RiftboundGG — the momentum bull
- Individual (~950 followers; Challenger TFT/League player, posts "Stocks, TCGs, UFC"). **Not affiliated with the riftbound.gg website** (that's a DotGG-network wiki — the handle squats the name).
- Angle: perma-bull on out-of-print promo scarcity. Runs a "Today's buy of the day" format. Recovered quotes: *"Nexus Night Origin packs. Undervalued, out of print, and exclusive… only way you can get the GGEZ Teemo"*; *"As soon as these get any demand they're going to go parabolic. The supply of these are just non existent"*; bull-case thread: *"KDA coming back and there being a set based around KDA… Faker signature cards/T1 cards… League esque cinematics"*.
- **Track record check**: his Nexus Night call verified directionally ($18/pack Jun 9 → $40+ on TCGplayer by Jul 6), and his T1/K/DA bull-case items did materialize. But he is always bullish, never posts exits or misses, and hashtag-farms. Useful as an idea feed, not as conviction.

- Anonymous, numbers-driven, focused on price comparisons. Its claims cross-check well.

### @smertcollector — the top-end showcase
- Graded-card flex account (e.g., Dec 2025: *"Jinx Signature 301* BGS 10 — POP 3 — 1 of 12 Signature Cards"*). Not a thesis poster; useful as a window into what the high-end collects and what low-pop grades exist.

### @_max94_ and @RiftCubTCG — no recoverable footprint
- Both exist but have effectively zero search-indexed Riftbound content (too small/new). Nothing to verify against. Watch what they post rather than assuming credibility either way.

## How to use this cluster

1. Treat **@Riftvesting** as data, **@RiftboundGG** as an idea feed requiring independent verification, **@smertcollector** as top-end market color.
2. Their **shared blind spot is reprint risk** — none of them stress it. Cross-check every "buy" call against: can Riot reprint this? If yes, discount heavily.
3. The **EU arbitrage** is the most concrete alpha recovered from the whole cluster — see CardTrader API integration in `data-sources.md` for automating it.
4. Add the pattern to your process: promo/event-exclusive items (Nexus Night, GG EZ Teemo, LNY packs, Worlds bundles) are where this community's verified wins keep coming from. That is consistent with — not contrary to — the reprint-risk thesis in `strategy-playbook.md`.
